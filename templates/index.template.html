Why this exists

Sauna specifications are unusually hard to compare.

Retail product pages often emphasize different fields. One model might lead with EMF terminology, another with heater output, and another with exterior dimensions. Infinite Sauna normalizes those fields into one schema so users can compare models without opening a dozen tabs.

Fields are populated from structured catalog data and product-page text. Manufacturer sources can supplement missing specifications, but automated extraction never silently overwrites a verified existing value.

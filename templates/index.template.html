Verification standard

Missing data stays missing.

Infinite Sauna gives priority to manufacturer manuals and technical documentation, then manufacturer product pages, authorized retailers and secondary retail sources. Ambiguous values are not published as facts.

“Not verified” means the current sources do not support a model-specific value. “Not applicable” means the field does not apply to that heat system. Manufacturer claims remain labeled as manufacturer terminology.
